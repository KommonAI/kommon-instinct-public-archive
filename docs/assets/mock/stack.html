Identity and messaging

Inkbox

Gives the agent its own phone number, iMessage line, email address and an agent-to-agent endpoint. Signs every inbound webhook. Other people's agents reach yours through it.

Hosting and the computer

One microVM per person with its own Linux desktop (Chromium, LibreOffice, files). Sleeps when idle, wakes in about a second. You take over the screen for logins and payments.

Agent loop

Pi

Runs the model, calls tools, keeps sessions. The policy guard sits in front of every tool.

Model

Claude

Fable 5.1 by default. Any provider in Pi's catalog, including local models.

Apps

Composio

Gmail, Calendar, Contacts and any other app, connected by the owner from a text.

Payments

Stripe Link

One-time cards for an exact amount, approved by the owner in Link before the agent pays.
