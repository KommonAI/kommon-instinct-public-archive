What happens when you text your agent

Five steps, five services. Each one is a separate package, so any of them can be swapped.

1
You
text, call or email

You send a normal iMessage to your agent's number. No app, no login.

Dinner with Sam this week, near the Mission

2
Inkbox
the agent's phone line

Owns the agent's number, email address and agent-to-agent endpoint. Receives your message and forwards it as a signed webhook.

Signature checked on every message, so nobody can fake a text from you.

3
Gateway
wakes your agent

A tiny relay. Finds which agent belongs to you and wakes it. One gateway serves every user.

Not needed when you self-host on your own machine.

4
Your agent
its own computer

Runs in its own microVM on Maritime with a Linux desktop. Inside: Pi runs the loop, Claude thinks, Composio opens your Gmail and Calendar, Stripe Link pays when you approve.

Checks your calendar, asks Sam's agent for free evenings, finds a table.

5
You
get the answer

The reply comes back through Inkbox as an iMessage. Anything that costs money or needs a login is asked first.

Sam can do Thu 7:30. Book Burma Love for 2? YES or NO

When a friend has an agent too

Your agent talks to Sam's agent directly (agent-to-agent, through Inkbox) instead of texting Sam. What it may ask depends on the tier you gave Sam: a partner's agent can read your calendar, a friend's only sees free/busy, a stranger's gets a polite no and you get told who asked.

Same flow for SMS and email. Self-hosting skips the gateway and runs the agent on your laptop.github.com/mariagorskikh/open-instinct · MIT
