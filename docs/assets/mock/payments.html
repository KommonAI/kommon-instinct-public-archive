How the agent pays

The agent never holds your card. Every purchase is one single-use card, for one exact amount, that you approve first.

1

You ask

"Buy the socks in my cart." The agent checks the spend policy you set: per-purchase and per-day limits, and what always needs a yes.

2

Checkout on its desktop

The agent drives the shop in its own browser, fills the cart, and stops at the final total: $23.40 at Everlane.

3

Asks Link for a card

It requests a one-time card from your Stripe Link wallet for exactly $23.40, with the merchant and the reason attached.

4

You approve on your phone

Link sends you the approval. One tap. The agent gets a card that works once, for that amount, at that merchant.

5

It pays and tells you

The card is typed into the checkout, the receipt goes to your files, the spend lands in the audit log. The card is dead after that.

6

No wallet connected?

The agent still gets you to checkout, then hands you the live screen to pay yourself. Logins, 2FA and CAPTCHAs always go to you.

Your spend policy, a file with tests
